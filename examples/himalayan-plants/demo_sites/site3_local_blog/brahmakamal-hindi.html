ब्रह्मकमल (Brahmakamal)

Saussurea obvallata

ब्रह्मकमल (Brahmakamal), or Saussurea obvallata, is the state flower.

Walkers find it around 9,842 ft on the high meadows.

It flowers in July.

We saw it above Garwhal, in Chamoli.

Villagers speak of its medicine and its use as a ritual offering.
