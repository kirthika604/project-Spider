Nardostachys jatamansi

Nardostachys jatamansi

Nardostachys jatamansi, called Jatamansi, is an aromatic rhizomatous herb.

It grows at 3,000 to 5,000 m.

Flowering occurs in July.

The species is reported from Uttarkashi district in Uttarakhand.

The rhizome is valued in medicine and as a perfume.
