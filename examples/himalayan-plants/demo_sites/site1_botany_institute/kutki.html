Picrorhiza kurroa

Picrorhiza kurroa

Picrorhiza kurroa, known as Kutki, is a bitter herb of alpine slopes.

It grows at 3,000 to 4,300 m.

Flowering is in June.

It is collected in Chamoli district and in Kinnaur.

The root is a well known medicine for liver complaints.
