Saussurea obvallata

Saussurea obvallata

Saussurea obvallata, commonly called Brahmakamal, is a perennial herb of the alpine Himalaya.

It grows at 3,000 to 4,500 m above sea level.

Flowering takes place in July and August.

The species is recorded from Chamoli and Uttarkashi districts of Uttarakhand.

The plant has medicinal value and is also used as a religious offering at Himalayan shrines.
