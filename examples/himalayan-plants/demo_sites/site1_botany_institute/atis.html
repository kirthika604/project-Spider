Aconitum heterophyllum

Aconitum heterophyllum

Aconitum heterophyllum, known locally as Atis, is a threatened medicinal herb.

It occurs between 2,000 and 4,000 m.

Flowering is in August.

It is found in Pithoragarh and Chamoli districts of Uttarakhand.

The roots are used in ayurvedic use for fever.
