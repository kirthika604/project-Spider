Saussurea obvallata

Saussurea obvallata

Saussurea obvallata (Brahmakamal) is recorded across the alpine zone.

Altitude range: 3,000 to 4,500 m.

It flowers in July.

Specimens are held from Chamoli, Uttarakhand.

Reported medicinal use in local practice.
