Rhododendron arboreum

Rhododendron arboreum

Rhododendron arboreum is a tree of temperate forests, called Burans locally.

Flowering is in March.

It is common through Almora and Nainital, Uttarakhand.

The flowers are used to make a drink, and the wood is used as fuelwood.
