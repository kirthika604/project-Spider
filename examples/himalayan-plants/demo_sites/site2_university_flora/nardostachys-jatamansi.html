Nardostachys jatamansi

Nardostachys jatamansi

Nardostachys jatamansi is an aromatic herb of the subalpine zone.

Altitude range: 2,800 to 4,000 m.

Flowering in July.

Collected in Uttarkashi and Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand.

Used as a perfume and in medicine.
